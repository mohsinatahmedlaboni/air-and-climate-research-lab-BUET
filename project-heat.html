Research / Project

Development of Mobile Air Quality Monitoring Unit for High-Resolution Air Pollution Mapping

A vehicle-mounted monitoring unit that maps air pollution at finer spatial resolution than fixed stations can provide.

Overview

The project builds a mobile air quality monitoring unit and uses its measurements to map air pollution street by street.

It covers development and calibration of the mobile system, high-resolution pollution mapping and model validation, machine learning and GIS-based air quality modeling, and field monitoring for empirical model development.

What the project does

01

Development and calibration of mobile monitoring systems

Building the vehicle-mounted unit and calibrating its instruments so its readings can be trusted.

02

High-resolution pollution mapping and model validation

Turning on-road measurements into fine-grained pollution maps and using them to check model results.

03

Machine learning and GIS-based air quality modeling

Combining mobile measurements with spatial data to estimate air quality between measured streets.

04

Field monitoring and empirical model development

Field campaigns that collect the measurements behind the project's empirical models.

Monitoring routes

Routes driven by the mobile monitoring unit to build high-resolution pollution maps.
