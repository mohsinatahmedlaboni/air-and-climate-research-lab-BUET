        About

        The Air and Climate Research Lab (ACRL) at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology focuses on atmospheric science, air quality and climate. Field observations, environmental monitoring, laboratory analysis and computational modeling are combined to improve understanding of air pollution and regional atmospheric conditions.
