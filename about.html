About the lab

Facilitating a clearer picture of air pollution and climate management in Bangladesh.

Who we are

Air quality, atmospheric science and climate research at BUET

The Air and Climate Research Lab (ACRL) at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology works on atmospheric science, air quality and climate.

It combines field observations, environmental monitoring, laboratory analysis and computational modeling to improve understanding of air pollution and regional atmospheric conditions. [Add: when the lab was founded, and its mission in one line]

How we work

Field observations

Measurements across urban, rural and near-source sites in Bangladesh.

Environmental monitoring

Reference-grade, chemically speciated and low-cost sensor networks.

Laboratory analysis

Chemical analysis of PM₂.₅ to identify pollution sources.

Computational modeling

Emission inventories, chemical transport and data-driven models.
