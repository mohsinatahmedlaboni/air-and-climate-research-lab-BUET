Atmospheric science, air quality and climate research at BUET

ACRL combines field observations, environmental monitoring, laboratory analysis and computational modeling to improve understanding of air pollution and regional atmospheric conditions in Bangladesh.

About the lab

What we work on

Our research
Emissions

Emission inventories

High-resolution estimates of emissions from major and minor sources, mapped across the country.

Models

Air quality modeling

Chemical transport and reduced-complexity models to test policies before they are put in place.

Sources

Source apportionment

Using the chemistry of PM₂.₅ to find out where pollution comes from.

Sensors

Monitoring and exposure

Monitoring across urban, rural and near-source sites, with mapping of population exposure.

People

Capacity building

Training regulators, students and professionals, and opening data to the public.
