Projects

Developing Tools to Improve Air Quality Management in Bangladesh

Building the emission inventory, air quality models, monitoring data and local expertise Bangladesh needs to plan and track its national clean air program.

Scope of work

  1. 01National emission inventory
  2. 02National source-resolved air pollution intervention model
  3. 03Chemically speciated PM₂.₅ monitoring and source apportionment
  4. 04Nationwide low-cost sensor measurements and data-driven air quality models
  5. 05Knowledge to action: training for Department of Environment staff and local professionals, public data and community outreach

Partners

HEAT sub-project · PIN 13125

Development of Mobile Air Quality Monitoring Unit for High-Resolution Air Pollution Mapping

A vehicle-mounted monitoring unit that maps air pollution at finer spatial resolution than fixed stations can provide.

Scope of work

  1. 01Development and calibration of mobile monitoring systems
  2. 02High-resolution pollution mapping and model validation
  3. 03Machine learning and GIS-based air quality modeling
  4. 04Field monitoring and empirical model development
